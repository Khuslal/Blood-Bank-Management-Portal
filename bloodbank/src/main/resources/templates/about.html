About LifeDrop

LifeDrop Blood Bank Management System connects voluntary blood donors, hospitals, and blood donation centers on a single platform to make blood donation and requests fast and transparent.

Our Mission

To ensure that no patient ever has to wait for safe blood due to lack of information or access.

How It Works

Donors register their blood group, donate at partner centers, and their donation is logged. Recipients can check live stock and submit requests, which are reviewed and fulfilled by center staff.

Safety First

All donations follow standard eligibility checks including a minimum gap of 90 days between donations.
